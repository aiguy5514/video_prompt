The 10-Second Reference-to-Video Framework

How to achieve perfect temporal consistency and zero identity morphing in modern video diffusion models.

Step 01 · First Frame Anchor

Attach Reference Image

In Kling AI, Runway Gen-3, Luma Ray 2, or Hailuo Minimax, upload your reference photo into the First Frame / Image-to-Video slot. The AI anchors facial structure, hair color, and wardrobe from frame 0.

Step 02 · 10s Choreography

Direct the 3 Temporal Beats

A static prompt causes video models to freeze or hallucinate. Kinetix structures your prompt into Beat 1 (0-3s Establish), Beat 2 (3-7s Dynamic Apex), and Beat 3 (7-10s Cadence Settle).

Step 03 · Temporal Guardrails

Copy Prompt & Exclusions

Copy the assembled prompt and negative video exclusions. Strict directives prevent limb hallucination, face warping, unnatural hyper-speed jitter, and plastic AI skin across all 240/300 frames.
